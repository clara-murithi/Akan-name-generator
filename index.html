The Akan are one of the largest ethnic groups in West Africa, living mainly in Ghana and parts of Côte d'Ivoire.They are known for their rich cultural heritage, which includes colorful festivals, traditional music and dance,kente cloth weaving, gold craftsmanship, and Adinkra symbols.

Historically, the Akan were influential in the gold trade and established powerful kingdoms, particularly the Ashanti Kingdom, making them one of the most culturally and historically significant ethnic groups in West Africa.

The Akan speak several related languages, such as Twi and Fante, and traditionally follow a matrilineal system in which family lineage and inheritance are traced through the mother's side.
